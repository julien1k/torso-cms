# Privacy

This page covers two things: this website, and the Torso package you install in your own project.

## In short

This site has no accounts, no analytics scripts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Its fonts and scripts are served from the site itself, not from a third party. Reading it does not require you to give any personal information.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., based in the United States. When you open a page, Vercel processes the technical details of the request on my behalf: your IP address, the time, the page you asked for, and your browser type. This is needed to deliver the page and to keep the service stable and secure.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is running a website that is reliable and safe. The site itself keeps no logs of its own. Vercel keeps request logs for a limited time, as described in its privacy policy.

Because Vercel is a US company, this data can be processed in the United States. Vercel acts as a processor under a data processing agreement, and the transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and by the EU's standard contractual clauses.

## The demos

On the [demos page](/demos), the text you type is sent to this site's server so the real save step can run on it. The result is sent back to your browser. The text is not stored and not logged by the site. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: the demo cannot work any other way.

## Cookies

Visitors get no cookies. The editor at `/edit` is for the site's maintainer. It sets a session cookie only after someone signs in, and that cookie is strictly necessary for the sign-in to work.

## If you write to me

If you send an email, I use your address and what you wrote to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. I delete the exchange when it is no longer needed. Issues and pull requests are handled by GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to the personal data held about you, and for it to be corrected, deleted or restricted. You can object to processing that is based on legitimate interest, and you can ask for your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, an email to the address above is enough.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. The one responsible for this site is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA) in Ansbach.

## The Torso package

Torso runs inside your own app, on your own host. It does not send your content, your code or any usage data to its author or to any third party. When you save in production, your app talks to GitHub with the token you gave it, to commit the change to your repository. Nothing else leaves your server.
