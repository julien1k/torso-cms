# About

Torso is a visual editor for Next.js sites. You click a piece of text on your own page, change it, and the change is written back to the source file it came from. There is no content database, no schema to define and no separate admin app to host.

## Who makes it

Torso is written and maintained by Julien Kollmann, as an independent open source project. There is no company behind it. The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/julien1k/torso-cms), and so is every change made to it.

## What it costs

Nothing. Torso is free, and it is released under the MIT license, so you can use it in personal and commercial projects, change it, and ship it with your own work. There are no paid plans, no accounts and no hosted service. It runs inside your own Next.js app, on your own host.

## How finished it is

Torso is early. Next.js 16 with the App Router is what it is built and tested on, and support for other frameworks is experimental or missing. The [status page](/status) lists what works, what is experimental and what it cannot do yet.

## This site

This site is built with Torso and edited with it. Its source is in the same repository, under `examples/landing`, and is a working example of the setup the [docs](/docs) describe.
